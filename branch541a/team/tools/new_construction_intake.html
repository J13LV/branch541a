2
Borrower Profile
Basic qualification inputs — these gate program eligibility immediately
2.1
Are you a first-time homebuyer, or have you owned a home in the past 3 years?
NV HIP, FL Housing, TDHCA, and most DPA programs require first-time buyer status (3-year non-ownership rule). This single answer opens or closes the DPA door.
2.2
Are you a U.S. veteran, active duty military, or surviving spouse?
2.3
What is your occupation? Do you work in public service, education, healthcare, or law enforcement?
This determines eligibility for hero programs — FL Hometown Heroes, TX TSAHC Heroes, TVLB
2.4
Will there be a co-borrower on this loan?
2.5
What is your current residency / citizenship status?
ITIN and non-permanent resident borrowers open the Non-QM product lane — important to identify early as it changes the entire qualifying approach.

5
Construction Details
Product selection for new builds requires specific construction information
5.1
What type of home are you building or buying new?
5.2
What is the current status of the build?
Completed new builds (CO issued) are treated like standard purchases — DPA programs fully apply. Homes under construction require a construction or OTC product structure.
5.3
If building custom — is the land owned free and clear, financed, or still to be purchased?
5.4
Does the builder have a preferred or required lender they are pushing?
Some builders offer incentives for using their preferred lender — important to know and compare
5.5
Is this home intended as your primary residence or an investment property?
DPA programs require owner-occupied primary residence. Investment new construction uses a completely different product track (DSCR Non-QM).
