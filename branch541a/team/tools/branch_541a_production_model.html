Hiring triggers — based on Gissella pipeline load, not calendar
Don't hire on a schedule — hire when specific triggers are hit. Hiring too early burns overhead. Hiring too late means quality degradation you can't see until files start stalling. The trigger column below is the signal to act, not the month number.
Second processor (junior / processing assistant)
Month 8–10 · Phase 3
Gissella approaching 16+ active files consistently. This is the most critical hire in the branch. She becomes a senior processor / DPA specialist who handles complex files. The junior processor takes standard FHA + simpler DPA files under her supervision. Without this hire, pushing to 4 states causes closings to slip.
Trigger: Gissella at 16+ files for 3 consecutive weeks
Marelis — transition from Doc Chase to LO
Month 6–9 · Phase 3 start
Marelis is already shadowing LO tasks and on the Xinnix ORIGINATOR ASSIST track. Her transition to producing LO is the lowest-cost capacity add available — she already knows the pipeline, the borrowers, and the products. Timing depends on her license and readiness, not a calendar date. Target NV and FL files first under supervision before TX or AZ.
Trigger: Marelis licensed + Xinnix ORIGINATOR complete
Laura — Originator Assist track (part-time LO support)
Month 9–12 · Phase 3–4
Laura is earmarked for ORIGINATOR ASSIST training pending MLO license. Once licensed she can handle intake-to-pre-qual tasks, freeing Ana and Jose for complex DPA files. She stays in her intake bridge role for APG but adds LO support capacity on simpler files. This extends the team without a full new hire cost.
Trigger: Laura MLO licensed + ORIGINATOR ASSIST complete
Fourth LO (experienced DPA originator)
Month 15–20 · Phase 4–5
By Phase 4 you have three LOs and two processors. A fourth LO makes sense only if Gissella's split team can absorb the additional pipeline without quality loss. Target someone with existing TX or CO book who understands DPA — not a rate-and-term originator you need to retrain. Wrong hire here sets you back 6 months.
Trigger: Processing stable at 20+ closings/mo for 60 days
