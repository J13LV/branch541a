Why this market is exploding right now
The rate lock-in effect
Millions of homeowners hold first mortgages at 2.5–3.5% from 2020–2022. They have massive equity gains but will never give up that rate for a cash-out refi at 6.5%. The HELOC is their only path to liquidity — and they need someone who can explain it clearly.
Your DPA portfolio clients bought at 6.5–7%. Different math — but they still have appreciation and still won't want to restart their amortization clock. Same result.
Home prices appreciated sharply
NV, FL, TX, and AZ are all in the top-performing appreciation markets over the past 3 years. A borrower who bought a $320K home in Henderson in 2022 may be sitting at $385K today — $65K in new equity on top of their down payment.
Your exact geographic markets are among the highest-equity-gain states. Your portfolio is particularly well-positioned for this product.
Banks are pulling back — your opening
Large banks tightened HELOC guidelines in 2023–2024. Non-bank lenders and PRMG's Non-QM second mortgage products now fill that gap for self-employed, ITIN, and higher-DTI borrowers who banks routinely decline.
Your Non-QM Closed End Second Expanded Access serves exactly the borrowers the banks just rejected. This is where your bilingual + Non-QM positioning wins clean.
