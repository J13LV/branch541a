Scripts are not optional reading. They are the production interface between product knowledge and revenue. Every LO on this team must be able to deliver every script below without notes — naturally and confidently. Jose sets the standard. Ana matches it. Marelis trains toward it.
Core pitch scripts — memorize and own
The DPA opener — every first conversation
Used by: Jose, Ana, Marelis · Trigger: any buyer inquiry
JoseAnaMarelis
"Before we talk about your rate, I want to ask you something most loan officers skip: do you know about down payment assistance programs? Depending on your income and the property, we may be able to cover your entire down payment — and in some cases a big chunk of your closing costs — at no cost to you. This isn't a gimmick. These are state-funded programs I specialize in. Let me ask you a few questions and see what you qualify for."
Why this works: It reframes the entire conversation before rate comes up. The borrower is now thinking about saving $15–20K, not comparing your rate to a competitor. Use this on every single first call — even if you think they don't need DPA. Let the income question tell you, not your assumption.
HELOC pitch — past client outreach call
Used by: Jose, Ana · Trigger: Sales Boomerang equity alert
JoseAna
"Hi [name], it's Jose from PRMG — I helped you close your home on [street]. I was reviewing your account and wanted to reach out. Based on current market values, your home has appreciated significantly since you bought it, and I wanted to make sure you knew about an option that a lot of homeowners miss: you can access that equity without touching your mortgage rate. It's called a Home Equity Line of Credit — and the version I have available lets you lock individual draws into a fixed rate so you're not exposed to variable rate swings. No refinance, your first mortgage stays exactly as is. Do you have 10 minutes this week to hear how it works?"
Key phrase: "without touching your mortgage rate" — this is the objection-killer. Homeowners who bought at 6.5–7% are still protective of that rate compared to where they think rates might go. Emphasize preservation of the first lien in every HELOC conversation.
Piggyback pivot — over-income DPA decline
Used by: Jose, Ana · Trigger: buyer exceeds DPA income limit
JoseAna
"I have some good news and a different option. Your income is just above the threshold for the assistance program — but I have a structure called a piggyback loan that solves the same problem a different way. Instead of one mortgage, we do two — an 80% first and a 15% second — and that eliminates your mortgage insurance entirely. Your monthly payment ends up almost identical to what DPA would have given you, and you only need 5% down. It's actually a cleaner structure for someone at your income level. Let me run the numbers side by side right now."
Never let the DPA decline be the end of the call. The piggyback pivot keeps the deal alive and generates two origination transactions instead of zero. Train Ana on this script specifically — she handles the most borrower calls and will use it frequently.
ITIN Non-QM pitch — bilingual community
Used by: Jose · Trigger: ITIN borrower with equity or buying inquiry · EN/ES
Jose
"Muchas personas piensan que necesitan un número de seguro social para comprar una casa, pero eso no es cierto. Trabajo con un programa especial que acepta el número ITIN. No necesitas W-2 — usamos tus estados de cuenta bancarios para calificar. Si has estado pagando renta puntualmente, probablemente puedes calificar para comprar. La única diferencia es el programa que usamos. ¿Cuánto tiempo llevas en tu trabajo actual?" // In English: "Many people think they need a Social Security number to buy a home — that's not true. I work with a specialized program that accepts your ITIN. No W-2 required — we use your bank statements. If you've been paying rent on time, you can probably qualify to buy."
This script is your biggest competitive moat. No bank representative in your market is having this conversation in Spanish with ITIN borrowers. The referral loop from one closed ITIN purchase generates 3–5 referrals within the same community. Every SmartBee and notary relationship amplifies this exponentially.
Contractor referral partner pitch
Used by: Jose · Trigger: meeting a contractor / home improvement professional
Jose
"I work with a lot of homeowners in this area, and one thing I hear constantly is: 'I want to remodel but I don't have the cash.' The problem is most of them don't realize they're sitting on $80–100K in home equity they could tap in 3 weeks — without touching their mortgage. I have a HELOC product with a fixed-rate lock option that most banks don't offer. If you ever lose a job because a homeowner says they can't afford it, send them to me. I'll have them funded in 3 weeks, and you close the job. No referral fee needed — just the relationship."
Contractors are highly motivated referral partners because the HELOC directly enables their revenue. Unlike Realtor relationships where you're competing for referrals, contractor relationships are purely additive — they benefit every time you close a HELOC for their client. Warm, transactional, and high-volume.
