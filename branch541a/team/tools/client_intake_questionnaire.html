1
Purchase Goals
What the client is trying to accomplish — sets the entire conversation
1.1
What is the primary goal of this conversation?
¿Cuál es el objetivo principal de esta conversación?
1.2
What is your timeline to purchase or close?
¿Cuándo planea comprar o cerrar?
1.3
What matters most to you in a loan?
¿Qué es lo más importante para usted en un préstamo?
1.4
Have you heard about down payment assistance programs?
¿Ha escuchado sobre programas de asistencia para el pago inicial?
DPA awareness shapes how we open the product conversation. Someone who hasn't heard of DPA is often the most impactful discovery of their home-buying journey.
2
Property Preferences
What they want to buy — affects loan limits, program eligibility, and product routing
2.1
What type of property are you looking for?
¿Qué tipo de propiedad está buscando?
2.2
Intended use of the property?
¿Para qué usará la propiedad?
DPA programs require primary residence. Investment property routes to DSCR Non-QM — completely different product track.
2.3
What is your target purchase price range?
¿Cuál es su rango de precio objetivo?
2.4
What city or area are you looking in?
¿En qué ciudad o área está buscando?
2.5
How long do you plan to stay in this home?
¿Cuántos años planea quedarse en esta casa?
Staying less than 3 years may affect whether HIP forgivable or a deferred DPA second is the right structure — the second could come due before forgiveness.
3
Borrower Profile
FTB status · veteran · hero occupation · citizenship — these four answers unlock or close DPA programs immediately
3.1
Have you owned a home in the past 3 years?
¿Ha tenido una casa en los últimos 3 años?
First-time buyer status (3-year rule) opens NV HIP, FL Housing, TDHCA, and most DPA programs. This single answer is the most important gate in the entire interview.
3.2
Are you a U.S. veteran, active duty, or surviving spouse?
¿Es usted veterano, militar activo o cónyuge sobreviviente?
3.3
What is your occupation? Do you work in a public service or hero profession?
¿Cuál es su ocupación? ¿Trabaja en servicio público o profesión de héroe?
This determines eligibility for FL Hometown Heroes, TX TSAHC Heroes, and TX TVLB
3.4
Citizenship / residency status?
¿Estado de ciudadanía o residencia?
ITIN holders and non-permanent residents open the Non-QM product lane. No conventional DPA — but Non-QM Income Qualifying is available in NV and FL today.
3.5
Will there be a co-borrower?
¿Habrá un co-prestatario?
3.6
Is this your first generation of homeownership? Did your parents ever own a home?
¿Es usted la primera generación de su familia en comprar casa?
First-generation buyers may qualify for CO CHFA FirstGen Plus ($25,000 DPA) and other targeted programs
4
Income & Employment
How income is documented determines the product lane — W-2, bank statement, or ITIN Non-QM
4.1
How is your income documented?
¿Cómo documenta sus ingresos?
Bank deposits / limited tax docs = Non-QM Expanded Prime or Non-Prime. This is a complete product lane change — different qualifying, different rates, different comp.
4.2
Approximate combined gross annual household income?
¿Cuál es su ingreso bruto familiar combinado aproximado?
Most DPA programs have income limits between $80K–$160K. This single number opens or closes multiple programs.
4.3
How long have you been with your current employer or in your current business?
¿Cuánto tiempo lleva en su empleo actual o negocio?
Less than 2 years triggers employment history review — not a dealbreaker but needs documentation and explanation in the file.
4.4
Do you have any additional income sources?
¿Tiene otros ingresos adicionales?
4.5
Are you currently on an IRS payment plan (Form 9465)?
¿Tiene actualmente un plan de pago con el IRS?
Active IRS payment plans require 3 months of on-time payments and must be disclosed. Some DPA programs have specific requirements. Address this early.
5
Credit Profile
Score bands gate every program — 620 · 640 · 660 · 700 are the key thresholds
5.1
Approximate credit score? (middle score if you know all three bureaus)
¿Cuál es su puntaje de crédito aproximado?
620 = government/Non-QM opens · 640 = DPA programs open · 660+ = best pricing · 700+ = conventional DPA eligible

5.2
Have you checked your credit in the past 6 months?
¿Ha revisado su crédito en los últimos 6 meses?
5.3
Any significant credit events in the past 4 years?
¿Ha tenido problemas importantes de crédito en los últimos 4 años?
Non-Prime Non-QM accepts significant derogatory credit (settled FC/SS/DIL/BK). For ITIN borrowers with credit issues, we still have a path.
5.4
Current monthly debt obligations (car payments, student loans, credit cards, child support)?
¿Cuáles son sus deudas mensuales actuales?
High monthly debt directly impacts DTI and maximum purchase price. Over $1,500/month in obligations may require credit counseling or debt paydown before qualifying.
6
Assets & Down Payment
Available funds and their source determine how much assistance is needed and which programs apply
6.1
How much do you have available for down payment and closing costs?
¿Cuánto dinero tiene disponible para el pago inicial y los costos de cierre?
If DPA is available, this may be significantly reduced — but we need to know what you have available now
6.2
Where will your down payment funds come from?
¿De dónde proviene el dinero para el pago inicial?
Cash not in bank requires a 60-day paper trail. Business funds used for personal purchase need careful documentation. Flag these early to avoid underwriting surprises.
6.3
After the down payment, will you have 2–3 months of reserves remaining?
Después del pago inicial, ¿tendrá 2–3 meses de reservas?
Reserves = bank balance covering 2–3 months of the new mortgage payment. Some programs require this.
6.4
Do you currently own other real estate?
¿Tiene actualmente otras propiedades?

Current Housing Situation
Where they are now — informs timeline, motivation, and readiness to close
7.1
Where are you currently living?
¿Dónde vive actualmente?
7.2
What is your current monthly housing cost (rent or mortgage)?
¿Cuánto paga actualmente de renta o hipoteca al mes?
7.3
Have you started working with a real estate agent?
¿Ha comenzado a trabajar con un agente de bienes raíces?
7.4
Is there any urgency to your situation? (lease ending, life event, relocation)
¿Hay alguna urgencia en su situación actual?
8
Monthly Budget & Comfort
What the client can comfortably pay — critical for product and payment scenario conversations
8.1
What monthly mortgage payment would you be COMFORTABLE with?
¿Con qué pago mensual de hipoteca se sentiría cómodo?
This is different from what you qualify for — we want to know what feels right for your budget
8.2
Do you have any large upcoming expenses we should factor in?
¿Tiene gastos grandes próximos que debamos considerar?
New major credit purchases (especially vehicles) during the loan process can change the DTI and kill approval. This conversation needs to happen early.
8.3
Have you ever applied for a mortgage before?
¿Ha solicitado una hipoteca anteriormente?
8.4
Are you currently working with any other lenders?
¿Está trabajando actualmente con otro prestamista?
